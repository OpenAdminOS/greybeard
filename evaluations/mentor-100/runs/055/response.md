For Windows compliance:

- Define measurable requirements: supported OS versions, BitLocker, Secure Boot, TPM, firewall, antivirus/EDR, password posture, and acceptable device-risk level.
- Separate corporate, BYOD, privileged, kiosk, and exception populations.
- Pilot policies in report-only or non-blocking mode, then expand through staged rings.
- Pair compliance rules with conditional-access decisions carefully. Provide a grace period and remediation instructions before blocking access.
- Establish ownership for exceptions, expiry dates, approvals, and periodic review.
- Track compliance rate, evaluation errors, devices without recent check-ins, remediation time, and user-impact incidents.
- Test recovery scenarios, including enrollment failures, stale records, emergency access, and devices becoming noncompliant while offline.
- Document evidence retention and review cadence for audits.
