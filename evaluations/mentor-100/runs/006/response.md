Before changing a broadly assigned policy:

- Confirm the intended outcome, owner, approval, and maintenance window.
- Export or screenshot the current configuration and assignments.
- Identify included/excluded users, groups, devices, and dependency conflicts.
- Check for dynamic groups, nested membership, filters, and privileged/break-glass accounts.
- Estimate affected population and business-critical workloads.
- Review precedence, overlapping policies, licensing, and platform requirements.
- Test the exact change on a small representative pilot group.
- Define success signals, monitoring, rollback criteria, and rollback steps.
- Notify support teams and affected stakeholders.
- Use staged deployment rings, then verify results before expanding.
- Record who changed what, when, why, and the observed outcome.
