Windows compliance rollout checklist:

- Define compliance requirements, scope, owners, success criteria, exceptions, and rollback triggers.
- Validate policy settings against supported Windows versions and device-management prerequisites.
- Inventory targeted device groups, excluding unsupported, shared, break-glass, and business-critical devices as appropriate.
- Confirm user communications, support scripts, escalation paths, and remediation guidance.
- Start with a representative pilot group.
- Monitor policy assignment, evaluation results, false positives, user impact, and support tickets.
- Keep the pilot active for **48 hours**.
- Require **helpdesk review before expanding**.
- Record issues, adjust settings or exclusions, and repeat the pilot if acceptance criteria are missed.
- Expand through staged rings, pausing between rings to review compliance and operational impact.
- Maintain an approved exception process with owners and expiration dates.
- After full rollout, verify outcomes, document lessons learned, and schedule periodic policy review.
