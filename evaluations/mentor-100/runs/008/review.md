Use this structure:

**Change brief: [policy name]**

- **Decision requested:** Approve, reject, or revise [specific change].
- **Current state:** [Existing policy and who it covers].  
  Evidence: [policy document, owner, date verified].
- **Proposed state:** Replace “[current rule]” with “[new rule].”
- **Reason:** [Problem, trigger, or requirement].
- **Scope:** Included users, systems, locations, and data. Explicit exclusions.
- **Expected impact:** Benefits, affected workflows, user friction, cost, and dependencies.
- **Risks and controls:** Security, compliance, operational, accessibility, and rollback risks.
- **Implementation:** Owner, milestones, communications, training, enforcement date, and rollback plan.
- **Success measures:** Quantified outcomes, baseline, review date, and accountable owner.
- **Open questions:** List unresolved assumptions and required evidence.
- **Approvals:** Policy owner, legal/compliance, security, operations, and executive sponsor as applicable.
